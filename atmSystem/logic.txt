I would model the ATM as a Singleton since there is only one ATM instance in the system being simulated. An Account would contain details such as account number, holder name, balance, and a list of linked cards. A Card would contain the card number, PIN, expiry information, and a reference to its associated account. I would define enums for transaction types like BALANCE_INQUIRY, WITHDRAW, and DEPOSIT, and another enum for denominations such as ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, and ₹2000. A BankService would act as the backend and expose operations such as account creation, card issuance, PIN authentication, balance inquiry, withdrawal, and deposit. The ATM itself should not directly manage account balances; it should delegate those responsibilities to the bank service. For cash dispensing, I would introduce a CashDispenser component, potentially using the Chain of Responsibility pattern to dispense notes in different denominations and verify whether sufficient cash is available. The State Pattern is a good fit for ATM workflow management, with states such as IdleState, CardInsertedState, AuthenticatedState, and TransactionState, each controlling which operations are allowed. 

ConcurrentHashMap and synchronization around balance updates or ATM cash inventory is usually sufficient.
