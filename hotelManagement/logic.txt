I'm thinking of designing the Hotel Management System by introducing a Hotel class that maintains all rooms, reservations, guests, and staff members. The Room entity would preferably be an abstract class since all rooms share common properties such as room number, price per night, amenities, and status, with concrete implementations like SingleRoom, DoubleRoom, and SuiteRoom. I would maintain separate enums for RoomStatus (AVAILABLE, OCCUPIED, MAINTENANCE, CLEANING) and ReservationStatus (PENDING, CONFIRMED, CHECKED_IN, CHECKED_OUT, CANCELLED) because room availability and reservation lifecycle are different concerns. A Guest management service would handle customer creation and information, while a Reservation class would store booking details such as guest information, room details, check-in/check-out dates, and payment information. For payments, I would use the Strategy pattern with multiple implementations like UPI, card, and cash payments to keep the system extensible. I would also use the State pattern to manage the room lifecycle, transitioning through states such as Available, Reserved, Occupied, and Maintenance after checkout, allowing hotel staff to easily retrieve rooms that need cleaning or maintenance. To improve separation of concerns, I would introduce a dedicated SearchService to find available rooms based on room type and date ranges instead of mixing search logic with booking logic. Finally, I would use the Observer pattern to send email, SMS, or app notifications whenever a reservation is created, confirmed, cancelled, or completed.
