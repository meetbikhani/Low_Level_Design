In the Meeting Room Scheduler, we will have a MeetingService responsible for managing meeting rooms, scheduling meetings, and handling cancellations and rescheduling. Each MeetingRoom will have properties such as room ID, name, capacity, floor. We will have a User class representing meeting organizers and participants, and a Meeting class containing meeting ID, title, organizer, participants, selected room, start time, end time, and meeting status. When a user requests a meeting, they will provide the start time and end time, along with the required room capacity and amenities. The MeetingService will use a RoomSelectionStrategy to find a suitable room based on the requirements, with strategies such as selecting the first available room or the smallest suitable room. Before confirming the booking, the system will perform overlap validation by checking the requested time interval against existing meetings in that room. Two meetings overlap when newStart.isBefore(existingEnd) && existingStart.isBefore(newEnd), ensuring that a room cannot have two overlapping bookings while allowing back-to-back meetings. We will also validate the organizer's and participants' availability before confirming the meeting. Once the booking is successful, the meeting will be stored with a SCHEDULED status, and we will use the Observer Design Pattern to notify all participants about the newly scheduled meeting. The same observer mechanism can notify participants when a meeting is cancelled or rescheduled. We will maintain meeting statuses such as SCHEDULED, CANCELLED, and COMPLETED, allowing users to view their upcoming meetings as well as their past meeting history. The system will also support cancelling and rescheduling meetings, updating room availability and participant calendars accordingly. Finally, we will handle concurrent booking requests by making the availability check and booking operation atomic, preventing multiple users from booking the same room for overlapping time slots.
