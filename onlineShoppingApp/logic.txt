The Online Shopping System consists of entities such as Customer, Account, Product, Category, Cart, CartItem, Order, and OrderItem, along with services to manage products, inventory, carts, orders, and payments. Each account is linked to a customer, who can browse products by category, add products to their cart, update quantities, and place an order. The cart maintains a map of product IDs to CartItem objects containing the product and its quantity. When the customer places an order, the OrderService validates the cart, checks product availability through the InventoryService, reserves the required stock, and coordinates payment processing. Once payment succeeds, the order is confirmed and the inventory is updated; if payment fails or times out, the reservation can be released. The Order maintains its lifecycle using the State Pattern, with states such as PLACED, CONFIRMED, DISPATCHED, DELIVERED, and CANCELLED, ensuring that only valid state transitions are allowed. An Observer Pattern can notify customers about order confirmation, dispatch, delivery, payment status, or cancellation through email, SMS, or app notifications. The system should also handle concurrent purchases safely so that multiple customers cannot purchase the same remaining stock, and each order should preserve a snapshot of the product details and prices at the time of purchase.
