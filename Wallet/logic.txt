I'm thinking of designing the Digital Wallet System by introducing a DigitalWallet singleton that manages multiple users, their accounts, payment methods, and transactions. Each user can own multiple accounts in different currencies, making multi-currency support cleaner instead of storing a single balance per user. Every account will maintain its own balance, currency type, and transaction history, where a Transaction entity stores details such as sender, receiver, amount, timestamp, and transaction status. For payment processing, I would use the Strategy pattern by creating an abstract PaymentMethod class with implementations like CreditCard, DebitCard, UPI, and NetBanking, each maintaining its own properties such as CVV, IFSC code, limits, or available balance, and validating funds before processing payments. Currency conversion responsibilities would be delegated to a separate CurrencyConverterService that converts amounts during transfers between accounts with different currencies. To keep operations thread-safe, methods like deposit, withdrawal, and money transfer can be synchronized. I would also introduce a WalletService to encapsulate business operations such as creating users, adding payment methods, transferring money, and retrieving transaction history. Finally, I would use the Observer pattern to send email, SMS, or push notifications whenever a transaction succeeds or fails, making the system more event-driven and extensible.
