The Food Delivery System consists of entities such as User, Restaurant, DeliveryAgent, Menu, MenuItem, Order, Cart, Address, and Payment. The system can be initialized using a Singleton FoodDeliverySystem, through which users, restaurants, and delivery agents can be registered and managed. Each restaurant maintains its own menu containing different items, prices, and availability. Users can browse restaurants and search using the Strategy Pattern, with different strategies for searching by city, distance, or menu items. They can view restaurant menus, add items to their cart, and place orders. The OrderService validates the cart, checks item availability, calculates the total amount, and coordinates payment through a PaymentStrategy supporting different payment methods such as UPI, cards, and wallets. Once the order is confirmed, a DeliveryAssignmentService finds the nearest available delivery agent based on their current location and assigns the order to them. The State Pattern manages the order lifecycle through states such as PLACED, CONFIRMED, PREPARING, READY_FOR_PICKUP, PICKED_UP, DELIVERED, and CANCELLED, ensuring only valid state transitions are allowed. Finally, the Observer Pattern notifies customers, restaurants, and delivery agents about important order updates, such as confirmation, preparation, pickup, and delivery.
