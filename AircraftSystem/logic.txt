I'm thinking of designing the Airline Management System by introducing separate Flight and Aircraft classes, where an aircraft stores details such as aircraft ID, model, total seats, and seat configurations, while a flight maintains information like flight ID, source, destination, departure time, arrival time, and the aircraft assigned to it. Each flight will have a fixed set of seats, and users can search for available flights through a dedicated FlightSearchService, which supports filtering based on source, destination, date, and other criteria. A User class will store passenger information along with their booking history, allowing users to view all their past and upcoming reservations. The booking process will be handled through Booking and BookingManagement classes, where bookings are created, managed, and linked to users and flights. For payments, I would use the Strategy pattern by creating an abstract PaymentMethod class with implementations such as credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, and digital wallet payments, making the system easily extensible for new payment options. To improve user experience, I would use the Observer pattern to send email, SMS, or push notifications whenever a booking is confirmed, cancelled, or if a flight gets delayed or rescheduled. The system would also maintain transaction histories for payments, and booking operations would be made thread-safe using synchronization to ensure that two users cannot reserve the same seat simultaneously.
