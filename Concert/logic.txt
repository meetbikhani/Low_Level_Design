I'm thinking of designing the Concert Booking System by introducing a central ConcertBookingSystem that manages concerts, venues, users, bookings, and payments. A Concert class would store details such as artist, schedule, and venue, while a separate Venue class would maintain the seating layout. Each Seat would have its own seat number, type, price, and booking status such as available, or booked. Users can search for concerts through a dedicated ConcertSearchService based on artist, venue, city, or date, and reserve seats by creating a Booking object that stores the selected concert, user, seats, and booking status. Payments would be implemented using the Strategy pattern, allowing multiple payment methods such as UPI, card, wallet, and net banking. I would also use the Observer pattern to notify users through email, SMS, or push notifications whenever a booking is confirmed, cancelled, or if the concert schedule changes. Finally, after a successful booking, a Ticket object containing seat information and a QR code can be generated.
